02 — How it works

Four steps from plan to home.

  1. 1

    Plan at the base

    Place the habitat and the science points on the map of a 2 × 2 km zone at Mount Sharp.

  2. 2

    Check before going out

    S.E.R.A builds the route, keeps a 15 % suit reserve, checks sunset and live space weather, and gives the verdict.

  3. 3

    Watch for deviations

    During the walk S.E.R.A follows the plan. Off the route, it builds a new safe path to the next point. Mission Twin compares a fast and a gentle way.

  4. 4

    Return to safety

    On a NO-GO (storm, suit time, sunset) S.E.R.A rebuilds the fastest way home from where you stand. The way home is visible at every point.

S.E.R.A answers the challenge Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map: a hyper-local, layered view of one EVA site, built from several NASA missions, to plan and carry out a walk on Mars and do science on the way.
